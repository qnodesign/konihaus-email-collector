# Konihaus Print-Ready Logo Asset Pack

Included files:
- SVG: best master format for print vendors, embroidery digitizing, signage, and web.
- PDF: vector print format for professional printers.
- EPS: legacy vector format for some print shops.
- PNG: high-resolution raster exports at 1024px and 3000px.

Color palette:
- Forest Green: #264E36
- Warm White: #FFFFFF / Cream alternative #F4F0E8
- Gold Accent: #EBC363
- Dark One-Color: #1F2A23

Recommended use:
- T-shirt: centered chest mark or small left chest mark, white + gold on forest green.
- Polo shirt: embroidered small left chest mark. Use the one-color version if thread detail is limited.
- Mug: full-color logo centered, or one-color dark logo on white ceramic.
- Business card: gold foil dot with dark green outline, or white mark on forest green card stock.
- Tote bag: large one-color mark for clean screen printing.
- Cap/beanie: one-color embroidered icon for best durability.

Print notes:
- Keep the logo at least 25 mm wide for embroidery.
- For small embroidery, use the one-color version and simplify the gold dot if needed.
- Ask vendors to preserve stroke weight and rounded joins.
- Use SVG/PDF/EPS for production, PNG only for mockups or digital previews.
